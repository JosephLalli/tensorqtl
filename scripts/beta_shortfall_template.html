Why hapmixQTL's effect sizes fall short

Method status: half-read split adopted

On 29 September 2026, half-read split became the production association default: point-estimate ASE with Gibbs-informed weights, total expression log2((count + 0.5)/(effective library size + 1) × 106), unit total weights, and fitted residual scales. Existing expression PCs remain fixed.

This page preserves the earlier measurements. Here, split uses the original log2(CPM + 1) total phenotype; gibbs is the prior default with Gibbs weights in both channels. These results were not rerun under the new default. See the half-read comparison and implementation checks. Adoption accepts improved beta recovery with a precision tradeoff; it does not establish uniform precision improvement or SuSiE credible-set calibration.

    Definitions

    Simulated effect β
    The log2 allelic fold the plasmode simulates at one causal variant by binomial thinning of the haplotype carrying the lower-expressed allele by 2−|β|.
    Causal unit
    One (dataset, non-null gene) pair, scored at its causal variant; 150 per effect size and gene set.
    Count-scale truth
    β for the allelic and combined slopes; for the total slope, the least-squares slope of the exact log2 total fold on ALT dosage/2, which differs from β by the curvature of (1 + 2−β)/2.
    Pipeline-scale truth
    The noise-free simulated shift of each record on the pipeline's own scale (allelic log2 ratio with a 0.5 pseudocount; log2(CPM + 1) total), regressed on genotype without noise.
    CPM
    Counts per million: count × 106/effective library size (edgeR's library size × TMM factor). Adding 1 CPM adds effective library/106 reads.
    Inverse-variance combination
    The combined slope is the channel slopes averaged with weights 1/se², which is the most precise average only if both channels estimate the same quantity.
    Gene-clustered interval
    Genes resampled with replacement 2,000 times, each carrying all its units; the 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles of the recomputed mean.
    voom log-CPM
    limma::voom's transform, log2((count + 0.5)/(library + 1) × 106): a pseudocount of half a read rather than one CPM.
