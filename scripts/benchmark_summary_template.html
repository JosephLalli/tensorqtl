eQTL Method Benchmark

Which cis-eQTL method finds real effects in this cohort: the four hapmixQTL weightings, mixQTL, total-only tensorQTL, RASQUAL and TReCASE. Two tests answer it: effects planted into the cohort's own data, where the truth is known, and replication in 135 donors that no method saw.

Method status: half-read split adopted

On 29 September 2026, half-read split became the production association default: point-estimate ASE with Gibbs-informed weights, total expression log2((count + 0.5)/(effective library size + 1) × 106), unit total weights, and fitted residual scales. Existing expression PCs remain fixed.

This page preserves the earlier measurements. Here, split uses the original log2(CPM + 1) total phenotype; gibbs is the prior default with Gibbs weights in both channels. These results were not rerun under the new default. See the half-read comparison and implementation checks. Adoption accepts improved beta recovery with a precision tradeoff; it does not establish uniform precision improvement or SuSiE credible-set calibration.

Where things stand

The two tests

Planted effects (plasmode)

The cohort's real expression records are shuffled against the genotypes, which erases every real eQTL but keeps the real noise. Then one causal variant per gene is given a known effect in half of 100 genes, by removing reads from one haplotype. Methods are scored on how many of the planted genes they find. Two gene sets: the deep set (well-covered genes) and the low-coverage set (30 to 100 allele-resolved reads per donor).

Held-out replication (referee)

Every method maps the real 92 donors. Each method's top K genes are then checked in 135 other BrainVar donors: does the lead variant show the same direction of effect there at p < 0.05? Taking the same K from every method means an overconfident p-value cannot help. The referee sees only total expression.

Power is the share of the 150 planted gene units (50 genes × 3 datasets) a method calls when its ranking is cut where 5% of its calls are genes with no planted effect. It moves in steps of 1/150 = 0.007 and has no interval, so differences of one or two hundredths are not meaningful. Bars run from 0; the hairline marks tensorQTL.

Giving TReCASE its own inputs

RASQUAL and TReCASE are written for read counts from alignments, not Salmon estimates. So TReCASE was also run on counts from the RNA BAMs (featureCounts totals and phASER allele counts), with the same planted effects. Those allele counts were rebuilt twice: first per RNA strand at exonic SNVs with the HLA genes and hard-to-map regions excluded, then with WASP, which drops reads whose mapping depends on the allele they carry. The split weighting run on the same counts is the control.

Terms

hapmixQTL
This project's method: it combines total expression with allele-specific expression from Salmon on each donor's personalized transcriptome, weighting each donor by how uncertain its measurement is.
gibbs, split, unit, 1/(v+1)
The four hapmixQTL weightings. v is a donor's Gibbs variance (Salmon's measurement uncertainty). gibbs weights by 1/v in both channels and was the default for these recorded runs; split uses 1/v for the allelic channel and equal weights for total expression; unit uses equal weights; 1/(v+1) damps the largest weights.
mixQTL
The published method hapmixQTL extends, run from Salmon point estimates with its published read cutoffs, and with permissive cutoffs.
tensorQTL (total only)
Standard eQTL mapping on total expression alone; the baseline.
RASQUAL, TReCASE
Joint likelihood models of total and allele-specific read counts.
Planted effect
log2 fold change: 0.2, 0.4 and 0.8 are 1.15-, 1.32- and 1.74-fold.
eigenMT
A correction that multiplies a gene's best p-value by its number of effectively independent tests, estimated from the correlation between its variants.
